Sudoku Battle Rules

1. Take Turns: Players take turns placing a digit (1-9) onto any empty cell on the standard 9×9 Sudoku grid.

2. The Losing Condition: Whoever makes a move that results in zero possible valid completions (i.e., breaks the puzzle or makes it unsolvable) loses the game immediately!

3. CPU AI Levels:

  • Easy: Plays random valid moves.
  • Medium: Prefers Box Hidden Singles; falls back to random.
  • Hard: Prefers Line (Row/Col) and Box Hidden Singles.
  • Expert: Also utilizes Naked Singles logic.
