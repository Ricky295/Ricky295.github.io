Sun emulator

A slow, natural sunrise or sunset for your LIFX bulbs.

1Connect to LIFX

Get a token from cloud.lifx.com/settings. It's used only in your browser to call the LIFX API directly — nothing is sent anywhere else.

2Choose lights

3Sunrise or sunset

4When it happens

We'll try to use your browser location automatically.

5Animation

Shift color temperature with brightness
On supported bulbs, warms from 4000K down to the warmest tone they support as brightness falls (or the reverse for sunrise).

6Run it

Waiting to start…
Runs entirely in this browser tab. Keep this tab open while a schedule is armed or an animation is running.
