How it works: there are 9 sub-sudokus (S1–S9), plus one master sudoku overlaid across all of them. Quadrant i of the master board is the same physical cells as quadrant i of sub-board Si — shown with a subtle orange outline when you're viewing that sub-board. Use the mini-grid on the left to jump into any sub-board, or press M to return to the master grid. Every board, including the master, must independently follow standard sudoku rules, and the puzzle is solved the moment the master board is complete. Clue counts are prioritized on the master grid first — Easy leaves 15 master clues, Medium 10, Hard 5, and Brutal strips it to 0, filling in only from what the sub-boards force. Pick Custom to set any master clue count from 0 to 81 yourself. Extreme replaces the 9 sub-boards with 81 of them (S11–S99, one per master cell) — each extreme board contributes just its own single linked cell to the master grid, so the master is only ever solved by cross-referencing eighty-one separate 9x9 puzzles. Generating an Extreme puzzle carves 81 boards down to a unique solution each, so it can take significantly longer than the other difficulties — give it a few seconds. Conflicts are always checked live; the Check button additionally flags any filled cell that doesn't match the actual solution.
