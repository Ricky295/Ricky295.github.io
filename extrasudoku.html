How it works: there are 9 sub-sudokus (S1–S9), plus one master sudoku overlaid across all of them. Quadrant i of the master board is the same physical cells as quadrant i of sub-board Si — shown with a subtle orange outline when you're viewing that sub-board. Use the mini-grid on the left to jump into any sub-board, or press M to return to the master grid. Every board, including the master, must independently follow standard sudoku rules, and the puzzle is solved the moment the master board is complete. Clue counts are prioritized on the master grid first — Easy leaves 15 master clues, Medium 10, Hard 5, and Brutal strips it to 0, filling in only from what the sub-boards force. Pick Custom to set any master clue count from 0 to 81 yourself.
