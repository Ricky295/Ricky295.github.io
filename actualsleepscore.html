The sleep score Apple should have shipped

Apple's sleep score gives duration 50 points, bedtime consistency 30 points, and interruptions 20 points — deep sleep gets zero. Paste in your Health app stage data below and see what your night actually scores when depth counts. Physical index prioritizes deep sleep, cognitive index prioritizes REM, blended 2:1.

Find your stage minutes in the Health app: Health → Browse → Sleep → tap last night → Sleep stages. Apple shows Core (light), Deep, REM, and Awake minutes there — none of them feed into the score it shows you.
